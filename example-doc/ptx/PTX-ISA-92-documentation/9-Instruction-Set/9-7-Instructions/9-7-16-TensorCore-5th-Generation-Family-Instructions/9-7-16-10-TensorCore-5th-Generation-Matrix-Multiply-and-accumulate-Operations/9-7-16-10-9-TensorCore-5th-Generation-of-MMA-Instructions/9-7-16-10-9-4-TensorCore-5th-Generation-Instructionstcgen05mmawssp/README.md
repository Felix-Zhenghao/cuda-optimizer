# 9.7.16.10.9.4. TensorCore 5th Generation Instructions: tcgen05.mma.ws.sp

Documents `tcgen05.mma.ws.sp` combining weight-stationary convolution with sparse matrix A. Supports floating-point and alternate FP types with optional block scaling. Matrix A is sparse in Tensor Memory; shift and MMA operations combined. Requires PTX ISA 8.6+.
