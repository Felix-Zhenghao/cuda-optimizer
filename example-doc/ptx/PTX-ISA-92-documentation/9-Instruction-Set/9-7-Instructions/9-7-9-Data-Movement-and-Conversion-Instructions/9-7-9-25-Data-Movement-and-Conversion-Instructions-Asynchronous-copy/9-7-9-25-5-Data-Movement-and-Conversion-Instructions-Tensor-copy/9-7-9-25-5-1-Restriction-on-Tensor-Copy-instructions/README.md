# 9.7.9.25.5.1. Restriction on Tensor Copy Instructions

Documents restrictions on tensor copy instructions for sub-byte types (.b4x16, .b4x16_p64, .b6x16_p32, .b6p2x16), including unsupported operations, mandatory Box-Size/Tensor-Size alignment, coordinate alignment requirements, and memory alignment constraints. Also covers restrictions for 96B and 128B swizzle modes, .global.shared::cta direction constraints, and architecture-specific limitations for sm_120a and sm_103a.
