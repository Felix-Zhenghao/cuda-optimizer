# 9.7.18.2.1. SIMD Video Instructions: vadd2, vsub2, vavrg2, vabsdiff2, vmin2, vmax2

Documents dual half-word SIMD video arithmetic instructions. Performs two-way parallel add, subtract, average, absolute difference, min, or max on half-word elements selected from source operands. Supports optional saturation, secondary SIMD merge, or accumulate operations with configurable mask. Requires `sm_30`+, PTX ISA 3.0.
