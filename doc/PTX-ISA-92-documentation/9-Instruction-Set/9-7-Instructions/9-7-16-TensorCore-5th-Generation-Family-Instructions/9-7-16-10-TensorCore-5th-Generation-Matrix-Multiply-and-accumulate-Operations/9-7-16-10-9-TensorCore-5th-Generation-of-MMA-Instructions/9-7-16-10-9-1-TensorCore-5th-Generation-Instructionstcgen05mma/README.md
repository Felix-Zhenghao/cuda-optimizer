Documents `tcgen05.mma`, the core 5th-generation matrix multiply-accumulate instruction. Supports dense A (from shared memory descriptor or Tensor Memory) and B (from shared memory descriptor). Variants include standard MMA (`.kind::f16`, `.kind::tf32`, `.kind::f8f6f4`), block-scaled MMA (`.kind::mxf8f6f4`, `.kind::mxf4`, `.kind::mxf4nvf4`), and convolution MMA with `.ashift`/`.collector_usage`/`.i8` integer types.
