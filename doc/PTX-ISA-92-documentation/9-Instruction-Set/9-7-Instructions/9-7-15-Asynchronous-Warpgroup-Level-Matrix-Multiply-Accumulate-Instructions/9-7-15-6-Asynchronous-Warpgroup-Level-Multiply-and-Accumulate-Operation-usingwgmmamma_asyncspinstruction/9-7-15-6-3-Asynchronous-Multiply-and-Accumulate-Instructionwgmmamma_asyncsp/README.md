The `wgmma.mma_async.sp` instruction performs asynchronous sparse warpgroup MMA (`D = A*B + D`) where matrix A is structured sparse. Supports f16, bf16, tf32, FP8 (e4m3/e5m2), and int8 types with 2:4 or 1:2 sparsity. Requires sparsity metadata (`sp-meta`) and selector (`sp-sel`) operands in addition to standard `wgmma.mma_async` operands. Introduced in PTX ISA 8.2, requires `sm_90a`.
